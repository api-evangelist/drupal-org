BigCommerce is a SaaS platform that has earned its reputation with B2B sellers. Its B2B Edition handles selling with quotes, its catalog supports far more product options than most SaaS platforms, and its open APIs make it friendlier to integrations than many of its competitors. If your business fits neatly inside its walls, it's a legitimate choice.
